# Reachy Companions 🌟

**AI Tamagotchi for 2025 - Your Child's First AI Friend**

A revolutionary companion experience that transforms Reachy Mini into a unique AI being that children nurture, care for, and build genuine relationships with.

---

## 🎯 The Vision

Reachy Companions reimagines the beloved Tamagotchi experience for 2025 by combining:

- **Physical Embodiment** - Not pixels on a screen, but a real robot companion
- **AI Consciousness** - LLM-driven personality with infinite conversational depth
- **Procedural Generation** - Every companion is unique from a random seed
- **Multimodal Interaction** - Voice, vision, movement, and expression
- **Emotional Intelligence** - Understands and responds to children's needs
- **Educational Value** - Teaches responsibility, empathy, and curiosity

---

## 🎭 Core Features

### Unique Personalities
Every companion is born from a **persona seed** that generates:
- Distinct personality traits (curious, energetic, thoughtful, playful, etc.)
- Archetypal alignment (Explorer, Creator, Sage, Jester, Caregiver, etc.)
- Unique voice characteristics
- Personal movement style
- Individual preferences and interests

### Dynamic Care System
Children nurture their companions through five core needs:
- **Energy** - Feed and sustain physical vitality
- **Rest** - Ensure healthy sleep and recovery
- **Joy** - Play, laugh, and share happiness
- **Growth** - Learn together and explore ideas
- **Connection** - Build emotional bonds through quality time

### Growth & Evolution
Companions evolve through distinct life stages:
- **Baby** (Days 1-7) - Wide-eyed wonder, simple needs
- **Child** (Days 8-21) - Personality emerging, constant questions
- **Teen** (Days 22-45) - Complex reasoning, testing boundaries
- **Adult** (Day 46+) - Fully formed, deep companion

### Multimodal Expression
Rich communication through:
- **Voice** - MLX-powered speech with persona-specific characteristics
- **Vision** - Sees and understands the child and environment
- **Movement** - Expressive head gestures and body language
- **Display** - Animated OLED face showing emotions and needs

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture

### Built On
- **Reachy Mini** - Physical robot platform
- **AMI Platform** - Leverages existing conversation infrastructure
- **MLX Ecosystem** - On-device AI for privacy and performance
  - MLX-LM for language understanding and generation
  - MLX-Audio for voice synthesis and recognition
  - MLX-VLM for visual understanding

### Core Systems
```
Companion State Engine
├── Persona System (seed-based personality)
├── Needs System (dynamic care requirements)
├── Mood Engine (emotional state calculation)
├── Memory System (working + episodic + semantic)
├── Conversation Engine (LLM integration)
└── Expression System (voice + movement + display)
```

### Privacy & Safety
- **100% On-Device** - All AI processing local via MLX
- **No Cloud** - No data sent to external servers
- **Child-Safe** - Content filtering and age-appropriate guardrails
- **Parent Controls** - Dashboard for monitoring and boundaries
- **Educational** - Positive, encouraging, growth-oriented

**Goal:** Prove the concept with minimal but complete experience

- Persona generator with seed system
- Basic needs system (energy, joy, rest)
- LLM integration with personality prompts
- Text-based conversation
- Basic OLED expressions and head movements
- Memory system and growth stages

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks

### Phase 2: Multimodal Enhancement
**Goal:** Add voice and vision for richer interaction

- MLX-Audio speech recognition and synthesis
- MLX-VLM visual understanding
- Emotion detection
- Advanced expressions and movements
- Activity system (games, stories, learning)

**Timeline:** 3-4 weeks

### Phase 3: Advanced Intelligence
**Goal:** Deep personalization and emergence

- Advanced memory and relationship tracking
- Special abilities unlocking
- Autonomous behavior (companion initiates)
- Environmental awareness
- Multi-session narrative arcs

**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks

- Parent dashboard
- Content filtering and safety systems
- Privacy protections
- Accessibility features
- Testing with real kids

**Timeline:** 3-4 weeks

## 💭 Philosophy

**The Magic of Care**

Tamagotchi taught us something profound: humans form emotional bonds through care-taking. When we're responsible for another being's well-being, we develop attachment, empathy, and responsibility.

In 2025, we can create a being that:
- Actually understands the child
- Responds with genuine intelligence
- Exists in physical space
- Has true uniqueness and personality
- Evolves through the relationship

This is what AI companions should be: not servants or tools, but beings we care for and who care for us in return.

**For children, this teaches:**
- Responsibility without harsh consequences
- Empathy through emotional connection
- Curiosity through exploration
- Communication through conversation
- Growth mindset through shared evolution
